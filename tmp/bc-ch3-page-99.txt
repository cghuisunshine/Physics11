                                       The English scientist Robert Hooke  1635o1703  first noticed the direct proportion 
                                 between the force exerted on a solid object and the change in length of the object 
                                 caused by the force. If too much force is applied  and an object is stretched or 
                                 compressed excessively  the direct proportion breaks down. In that case  the object may 
                                 be permanently stretched or compressed. Hooke's law is written with force as the subject 
                                 of the equation:
                                                                              F = kHx

                                 where F is the applied force  x is the change in length  and k is the spring constant.
