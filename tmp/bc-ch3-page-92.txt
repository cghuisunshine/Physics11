                                           Surface area does not affect the force of friction appreciably. For example  it will 
                                    require the same force to slide a building brick on its edge  as it will on its broad side. The 
                                    two factors that have the greatest effect on friction are:
                                    1.  the normal force pushing the surfaces together  and 
                                    2.  the nature of the surfaces.
