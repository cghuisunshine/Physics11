                                         Newton studied situations involving forces between pairs of bodies and he stated 
                                  his conclusions about mutual forces between pairs of bodies as his third law. Newton's 
                                  third law of motion is also called the law of action and reaction.


                                      If two bodies interact  the force the first body exerts on the second body will equal 
                                     the force the second body exerts on the first body. The two forces will be opposite in 
                                     direction and will act simultaneously over the same interval of time.
